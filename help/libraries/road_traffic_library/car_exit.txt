CarExit


The CarExit block removes the car from the road network and passes the car agent into the regular Process Modeling Library flowchart where it can go through delays, queues, decisions, and so on.

Together with CarEnter, this block is used to model some of the car’s movement at a higher abstraction level, that is, without detailed physical-level road traffic modeling.
